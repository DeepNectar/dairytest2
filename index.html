📥 Import Template Format

Paste or upload your messages below. The name on every line decides who sent it — a message marked Deep is saved as sent by Deep only, and one marked 🫰🫶🏻Chota Don❣️❤️😘 is saved as sent by her only.

========================================
📖 COMPLETE DIARY - DEEP & 🍯🫰🫶🏻 Chota Don❣️❤️😘
========================================

### Date: [DD Month YYYY]   ← e.g. "### Date: 12 January 2026"
                          (all messages below belong to this day)

---

#### 🌅 Morning & Night Greetings        ← heading line, keep the ####

#1 - Message: [12 January 2026, 08:15 AM] - Deep: "Good morning! Did you sleep well?"
  What I felt: Felt so happy seeing her message first thing.
#2 - Message: [12 January 2026, 08:20 AM] - 🍯🫰🫶🏻 Chota Don❣️❤️😘: "Yes, had a crazy dream about us!"
  What I felt: Curious and excited to hear the dream.

#### 💬 Daily Life & Important Updates
#3 - Message: [12 January 2026, 01:05 PM] - Deep: "Office was busy, but thinking of you helped."
  What I felt: Wanted her to know she is my calm in the chaos.

#### 💖 Sweet Compliments & Love
#4 - Message: [12 January 2026, 09:40 PM] - 🍯🫰🫶🏻 Chota Don❣️❤️😘: "You always make me feel special."
  What I felt: My heart melted — that's all I ever want to do.

#### 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 About Kesha (Her Sister)          ← ONLY when Kesha is mentioned
#### 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 About Arya (Her Brother)           ← ONLY when Arya is mentioned
#### 🎯 Dreams & Future Plans
#### 🤗 Emotional Support & Care
#### 😄 Fun Moments & Jokes
#### 🔥 Flirty Banter & Teasing
#### 💕 Romantic Confessions
#### 💘 Attraction & Desire
#### 🌙 Intimate & Passionate Talks
#### 💝 Deep Chemistry & Connection

#### 💭 My Messages To Her (No Reply Yet)
#5 - Message: [13 January 2026, 09:00 AM] - Deep: "Hey, you didn't reply last night."
- Note: She hasn't replied to this message yet.
  What I felt: Waiting with my phone in hand — longing and a little worry.

#### 💌 Her Messages To Me                 ← her messages when I didn't reply
#6 - Message: [13 January 2026, 10:30 AM] - 🍯🫰🫶🏻 Chota Don❣️❤️😘: "Sorry jaan, was stuck in work!"
  What I felt: Even an apology from her feels like a hug.

---

### 📌 FINAL SUMMARY - COMPLETE DIARY
A normal day that became precious because she was in it. Morning greetings,
busy afternoons, her dreams and my waiting — every line pulled us closer.

========================================

Entry numbers: every - Message: [date] line may start with a running number — #1 - Message: …, 1. - Message: … or (1) - Message: … — counted 1, 2, 3 … till the end across all headings and days. Only the Message: [12 January 2026, 08:15 AM] … lines are counted; the What I felt: / Note: lines belong to the message above them and never get their own number. The number is just a label for checking big sheets — it is stripped before import and never stored in Supabase.
All 15 headings are supported. Use only the heading sections that have content — messages under each #### heading are automatically filed there. Skip the headings and they'll still be auto-detected from keywords. The 📌 FINAL SUMMARY block is saved as its own summary page in the booklet.
